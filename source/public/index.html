🎮 Build Your Own Jungle Game!

Do you have an awesome idea for a jungle game? Amazing! Here's how to make it happen:

  1. Dream it up! Talk with your friends and family about your game idea. Draw pictures of what it would look like. What animals are in it? What does the jungle look like?
  2. Design it! When you're ready, write down or tell a grown-up all the details:
    • What is your game about?
    • What does it look like?
    • What do the players do?
    • How do players win and lose?
    • What happens on the screen as they play?
  3. Submit it! Ask a parent or guardian to help you submit your game idea. Click the big button below to send it to the official VDMDD platform!
